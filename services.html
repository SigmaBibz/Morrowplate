Portion-size guide

Hand-size estimates

  • Palm of your hand = 3 oz meat or fish
  • Your fist = 1 cup cereal, rice or vegetables
  • Cupped hand = 1/2 cup beans or chopped fruit
  • Thumb = 1 tbsp dressing or cream cheese
  • Thumb tip = 1 tsp butter or sugar

Household object estimates

  • Deck of cards = 3 oz meat or tofu
  • Tennis ball = 1/2 cup fruit or vegetables
  • Baseball = 1 cup salad or cereal
  • Golf ball = 2 tbsp peanut butter
  • Four dice = 1 oz hard cheese
